The problems

The parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped: a booking for 7 April goes out as 4 July. This holds for every channel except the booking-v3 form. It is the problem #4471 was named after.

The booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints the hint MM/DD/YYYY. The tenants on booking-v3 write the day first, so their people follow the hint and type the month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks like the parser problem, a swapped day and month, but the cause is the hint. The parser fix does not touch it. The #4471 page already shows this group as “no ticket of its own”.

Some bookings move by exactly one day. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” No day and month swap in these: neither fix explains them. The desk had noted them as one-day moves and filed them under the parser. The Lisbon and Toronto report points at time zones; that is a lead, not a finding.

How they were counted

  • The ticket export pages overlap, so the same report can appear twice. Counted once per id.
  • The cause comes from the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run (src/lib/kb/causes.ts). Not from the intake tag, which is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and not from the subject line.
  • The one-day moves come from the desk’s own notes. Every report in #4471 was read to check that no other report describes a one-day move.
  • Each report is in exactly one ticket.
  • The table above is the desk’s own view (CauseTable), computed from the export at render time.

What follows
